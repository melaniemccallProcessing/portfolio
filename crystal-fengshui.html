Overview

I built and manage the full multi-platform e-commerce ecosystem for Crystal Fengshui, an online mineral and crystal retail business selling across Shopify, Etsy, and eBay. The system includes storefront administration, inventory controls, listing management, fulfillment workflows, CRM automation, SEO, and multi-channel sales analytics.

Role and Responsibilities

  • E-commerce operations owner and webmaster
  • Shopify storefront management and theme customization
  • Product listing creation and optimization across platforms
  • Multi-channel inventory control and SKU standardization
  • Order processing, packing workflow, and fulfillment oversight
  • CRM automation using Klaviyo
  • Marketplace SEO and keyword optimization
  • Customer support and messaging workflows
  • Data cleanup and product classification
  • Image editing and visual merchandising

Platform Architecture

  • Shopify: Primary storefront, theme edits, product catalog structure
  • Etsy: Marketplace listing management and SEO
  • eBay: Wholesale product catalog, clearance item management
  • CRM: Klaviyo email automation and segmentation
  • Inventory System: Multi-platform quantity sync and audits
  • Fulfillment: Picking, packing, QC, and label workflows

Key Tools/Integrations

  • Shopify Admin and Liquid theme edits
  • Klaviyo email campaigning and Shopify Workflows
  • Bulk listing templates and metadata structuring
  • Analytics dashboards and reporting exports
  • CSV import tools
  • Canva: Product and website photo editing
  • Judge.me Review Widget

Key Achievements

  • Built and operated a multi-channel e-commerce ecosystem
  • Reduced oversell risk through structured inventory controls
  • Improved listing efficiency using standardized SKU templates
  • Increased repeat customer revenue via CRM automation
  • Enhanced fulfillment efficiency through workflow organization
  • Improved merchandising using imagery, SEO, and data optimization
